Verified by you.

The tracker excludes listings with reported salvage, lemon, frame-damage or theft history. Owner counts appear only when explicitly supported; CarGurus search-filter matches are labeled unverified. Accident reported requires an explicit source statement or count; a missing history result is labeled History unavailable. Reported extras are marketplace claims, not VIN-decoded factory options. A rust-check marker means a truck is or was advertised in one of the selected states; it does not prove where it was registered or whether it has rust. No marker does not mean rust-free.
